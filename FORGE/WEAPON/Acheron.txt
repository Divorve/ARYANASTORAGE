📌 OVERVIEW
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  Weapon enhancement that supercharges your 3rd skill.
  Reward --> 2,000,000 Exp (quest can only be done once)
╰────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╯

📘 EFFECT
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  Skill 3 --> 💡 100% chance to activate (no cooldown)
              80% Magic Damage to up to 2 targets
              Always hits and crits

  🩸 Stygian --> Gain 1 stack for every target struck
                Stacks to 30 · lasts 6 seconds
                Rapid DoT based on max health, grows
                with each stack (it drains your HP!)
                +30% Damage Boost
                +30% Hit Chance
                (Only the DoT scales, other effects don't stack)

  Items required --> Dark Gem ×1 per turn-in
    Every turn-in gives:
    --> Dark Potion  (what you need ×20)
    --> Class Boost (60 Min) ×3
    --> Random gold / XP boost bags
    --> ONE random item from a list, which includes ???
      (the ??? (Misc) (2) item you need for Phase 3)

⚔️ PHASE 3 — THE VOID LODESTONE
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  Void Lodestone = Arcane Lodestone ×1 + Mercury Elixir ×1
  Merged at the Dark Loremaster's Shop  →  /join DoomWood

  [ A · Arcane Lodestone ]
  Map            --> /join Dreadfire (Dreadfire Keep)
  Quest          --> Open Ebony Chest
                     Click the Ebony Chest on Screen 6
  Requirements   --> ??? (Misc) (2) in your inventory
  Items required --> Golden Key (AC) ×1
                     Silver Key ×1
                     Bronze Key ×1
  Reward         --> Arcane Lodestone

  ⚠️ Silver and Bronze Key are dropped DURING the quest,
     so accept it first, then go kill for them.

  --> Silver Key ·····  /join Dreadfire  ·  Kill Living Brimstone
  --> Bronze Key ·····  /join Dreadfire  ·  Kill Stray Mana
  --> Golden Key (AC) ·  /join zazul  ·  Zazul Treasure Chest
                       (see maze guide below)

  [ 🔑 Zazul's House — Golden Key ]
  Screen 2  --> 5 doors with a changing counter
                Enter a door when the counter says "3"
                --> takes you to Screen 4
                (Counter "Z" sends you back to Battleon)
  Screen 4  --> Invisible maze → chest at the end
                Maze guide: https://imgur.com/li3YkzX
  The chest --> 4-digit passcode, 3 passwords
                Enter in this order, clicking "Open"
                after each one:
                1. Z, Z, Z, Z
                2. 1, 1, 1, 3
                3. 1, Z, Z, 1
                3 wrong attempts sends you to Screen 5
  Success opens the Zazul Treasure Chest shop → Golden Key (AC)

  [ B · Mercury Elixir ]
  Map            --> /join DoomWood (Doomwood Forest)
  NPC            --> Dark LoreMaster
                     ⚠️ Only visible with Arcane Lodestone in
                     your inventory
  Quest          --> Mercury Elixir
  Items required --> Mercury Elixir ×1 (temporary item)
                     --> /join BattleUnderB · Kill The Lost
  Reward         --> Mercury Elixir

  [ C · Merge the Void Lodestone ]
  Dark Loremaster's Shop  →  /join DoomWood
  --> Arcane Lodestone ×1
  --> Mercury Elixir ×1

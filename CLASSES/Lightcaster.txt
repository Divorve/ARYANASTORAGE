⚔️ QUEST
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  [ Step 1 — LightMage ]
  --> /join celestialrealm  ·  Purchase Evolved LightCaster armour from Aranx (1,000 AC — cannot be sold)
  --> Open LightMage Class Token A Shop → Purchase Token A
  --> Merge LightMage using Token A

  [ Step 2 — LightCaster ]
  --> Accept "LightCaster Class" quest from Aranx at /join celestialrealm
  --> Keep in your inventory: LightMage + the 5 weapons below
  --> Turn in: Aranx's Pure Light

  Burning Blade               ×1   → /join lostruinswar  ·  Kill Diabolical Warlord  (~1%)
  Burning Blade of Abezeth    ×1   → Merge at Ramiel (NPC, outside the boss room)  ·  /join celestialarena
                                     • 20× Champion Sash: accept "Final Fight", go through the middle portal, kill Aranx (100% drop)
                                     • 3× Gold Voucher 500k: Ramiel's merge shop, 500,000 Gold each
  Guardian of Spirits' Blade  ×1   → Aranx's quest  ·  500 Trapped Spirits from Fallen Knights (/join lostruins or /join lostruinswar)
  Avatar of Death's Scythe    ×1   → Aranx's quest  ·  500 Energy of Death from Underworld Hounds (/join lostruins or /join lostruinswar)
  Lance of Time               ×1   → Aranx's "Spear of Time" quest  ·  500 Captured Time from Infernal Imps (/join lostruins or /join lostruinswar)
  Aranx's Pure Light          ×1   → /join celestialarena  ·  Kill Aranx  (~10%)

  💡 Farm Aranx once for both: the 20 Champion Sashes and Aranx's Pure Light
     drop from the same fight.

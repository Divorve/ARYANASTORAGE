⚔️ SKILLS
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  [ 1 · Gilded Crusher ]     Rank 0 · 3s CD · Physical
  --> Auto attack. Damage scales with your current HP.

  [ 2 · Titanic Smash ]      Rank 1 · 4s CD · Physical · 💉 20% max HP
  --> Crippling Smash on target: -60% outgoing damage,
      -30% Crit Chance for 8s. 25% chance to stun for 1s.

  [ 3 · Titan's Fury ]       Rank 2 · 12s CD · Physical · 💉 20% max HP
  --> You: +20% Haste. Target (Disemboweled): +30% damage taken.
      Both last 15s.

  [ 4 · Titan's Curse ]      Rank 3 · 16s CD · Physical · 💉 10% max HP
  --> Taunt (Focus) for 4s. Titan's Curse on target:
      +5,000% Crit Chance but -300% Crit Damage for 2s.

  [ 5 · Blood Rage ]         Rank 5 · 20s CD · Magical · 💉 30% max HP
  --> Massive hit. +100% outgoing physical damage but
      -50% Heal Power for 5s.

  PASSIVES
  Crimson Shield    (Rank 4)  --> Damage taken -80%
  Blood Volume      (Rank 4)  --> Endurance +300%, Dodge -50%
  Titanic Bloodline (Rank 10) --> Skills can't miss or crit
                                  Skills 2–5 heal 20% max HP over 1s
                                  Gilded Crusher applies Life Drinker
                                  (5% max HP every 2s)

🔢 COMBOS
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  💡 Every skill costs HP, but each one also heals 20% max HP back
     (Titanic Bloodline) — Skill 2 is the cheapest refill on a 4s CD

  Skill 2 --> 💡 Your sustain button — near-free heal that also
              cuts enemy damage by 60%

  Skill 4 --> 💡 Forces the enemy to crit with -300% Crit Damage,
              so their hits heal you instead (2s window)

  Skill 5 --> 💡 Burst — but halves Heal Power for 5s,
              make sure HP is high before using it

  Main Rotation --> ✅ Spam 2 until ready → then 5 → 4 → 3 → 2
                    --> repeat cycle

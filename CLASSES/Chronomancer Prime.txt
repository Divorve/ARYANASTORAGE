  Worth Getting?
  ──────────────
  Yes — possibly the easiest chrono to use, with great
  overall output. Short nuke cycles with high damage.
  A great first pick chronomancer.

  Best Used For:  💥 DPS  |  🌍 Most game content

║            ENHANCEMENTS              ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════╝

  ✅ Damage  (9/10)
  ⚔️  Weapon ········  Dauntless
  🪖 Helm ··········  Anima
  🧣 Cape ··········  Vainglory
                       → Fast haste. Use 5 so you don't miss,
                         drop 3 (haste buff is already big)

  ⚔️  Weapon ········  Lacerate
  🪖 Helm ··········  Anima
  🧣 Cape ··········  Vainglory
                       → Lacerate lowers enemy dodge, so 5 is

╔══════════════════════════════════════╗
║               SKILLS                 ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════╝

  💡 Skill 2 ········  Temporal Strike
                        60% Hybrid · 1.5s cooldown (0.75s max haste)
                        Stacks Temporal Rift each hit
                        1st rift starts recording your damage
                        (last 10 seconds) for the nuke
                        → Spam it to stack rifts fast

  💡 Skill 3 ········  Rift Inversion
                        70% Hybrid heal · always crits
                        Heal scales with rifts (uses them up)
                        +15% Haste for 6s
                        12s cooldown (6s max haste)

  💡 Skill 4 ········  Rift Collapse  (the nuke)
                        Uses up your rifts · scales up to 4
                        80% crit chance · 12s cooldown (6s)

  💡 Skill 5 ········  Rift Burn
                        Applies a DoT · −15% enemy dodge
                        So your nuke doesn't miss
                        12s cooldown (6s max haste)

  ℹ️  Rift multiplier   1 rift = 0.5  ·  2 = 0.7
                        3 = 1.0  ·  4 or more = 1.5


╔══════════════════════════════════════╗
║               COMBOS                 ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════╝

  ✅ Dauntless / with supports
                        5 → 2 → 2 → 2 → 2 → 4
                        Skip 3, you already have a haste buff

  ✅ Lacerate ·······  (5) → 3 → 2 → 2 → 2 → 2 → 4
                        5 is optional, adds some DoT

  💡 Fewer 2s ·······  Low HP enemies don't need 4 rifts,
                        use fewer 2s before nuking

  💡 Max stacks ·····  You can stack up to 7 rifts before
                        the nuke's 10s recording runs out

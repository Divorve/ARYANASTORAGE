╔══════════════════════════════════════╗
║               COMBOS                 ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════╝

  💡 Skill 1 ········  Nightmare Gaze — applies Madness to your target
                        (stacks to 50, lowers their Crit and Dodge)

  💡 Skill 2 ········  Tentacle Slam — gives you Feeding (stacks to 10)
                        At 5 Feeding you gain Writhe (+25% resistances & healing)

  💡 Skill 3 ········  Tidal — crit hit + Red Tide
                        (lowers target's Damage Resistance, more per Madness stack)

  💡 Skill 4 ········  Frenzied Feast — needs 5+ Feeding stacks
                        +100% Crit Damage and Skill 2 damage
                        Skill 1 adds an extra Madness stack
                        ⚠️ Used with 1–4 Feeding = −200% Crit Damage for 3s

  💡 Skill 5 ········  Call of Kathool! — unavoidable crit, effect depends on Madness:
                         5  · −25% Physical & Magical Resistance
                        20  · −50% Outgoing Damage and Haste
                        50  · DoT + −100% Hit Chance
                        Effects last 15 seconds

  ✅ Main Rotation ··  4 → 2 → 3 → 2 → 2 → 4 → 5 → 3  → repeat

  👥 Buffing Team ···  Stack Feeding to 10 first (spam 2 before using 4)
                        At 10 Feeding, Skill 4 shares Writhe with your allies

  💡 Multiple Mobs ··  With more than 1 enemy, you can use 4 earlier

  ⚠️ Don't use 4 below 5 Feeding stacks unless you're still stacking

  Items required ···  Kathool's Morsel Consumed ×1
  Reward ···········  Kathool Adept (Class)

⚖️ PROS & CONS
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  [ ✅ Pros ]
  - Always hits and crits
  - Can work around Silence since skill 2 needs no mana
  - Strong in Sustained damage and Self-healing

  [ ❌ Cons ]
  - Mid-High Tier DPS (Slightly weaker than Chaos Avenger)
  - Needs to sacrifice damage to sustain/constantly swap between healing/dealing damage
  - Low damage resistance (20%, 40% if use Penitence)
  - Cannot withstand burst attacks
  - Cannot nuke/burst
  - Cannot proc with Ravenous or Elysium due to fairly long skill 3 CD

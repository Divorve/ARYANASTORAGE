📌 OVERVIEW
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  Also known as --> Immortal Chronomancer · Eternal Chronomancer (ECM)

  Worth getting? --> Yes, if you like it — not a meta chrono, but fun and
                     capable of big damage. Large nuke coefficient and a
                     decent nuke cooldown: great for deleting damage-sponge
                     mobs. Very good defenses for a chrono (30% damage
                     reduction + a decent HoT). Basic to use.

  Best for --> 💥 DPS · 🧱 High-HP mobs

✨ ENHANCEMENTS
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  ✅ Party DPS  (9/10)
  Class --> Luck
  Weapon --> Valiance               
  Helm   --> Anima
  Cape   --> Vainglory
             --> Best in parties. No haste worries,
             just go all in on nukes

  🔁 Solo DPS  (8/10)
  Class --> Luck
  Weapon --> Dauntless              
  Helm   --> Anima
  Cape   --> Lament or Vainglory

             even when using The Fourth Dimension

🔢 SKILLS
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  Skill 1 --> 💡 Time Strike
              100% Physical auto attack · 2s (1s)

  Skill 2 --> 💡 Eternal Deadlock (15 mana · never misses)
              60% Physical · 4s cooldown (2s max haste)
              Infinite Hit on target: −25% dodge for 5s
              +1 Deadlock (stacks to 8)
              At 8 → Enter Infinity: +20% crit for 3s
              (uses 7 stacks)

  Skill 3 --> 💡 The Fourth Dimension (15 mana · never misses)
              60% Physical · 4s cooldown (2s max haste)
              +1 Temporal Rift (stacks to 4)
              1st rift starts recording damage for the nuke
              +1 Deadlock (stacks to 8)
              At 8 → The Fourth Dimension: +20% Physical
              damage for 3s (uses 7 stacks)

  Skill 4 --> 💡 Speed Abrasion (the nuke · 35 mana)
              150% Physical · 16s cooldown (8s max haste)
              Scales with rifts + damage from the last 10s
              --> Best at 4 rifts with Fourth Dimension or
              Enter Infinity active. 2–3 rifts is fine
              for a quick kill

  Skill 5 --> 💡 Taking Eternity (10 mana · 6 allies)
              4s cooldown (2s max haste) · lasts 15s
              +30% Damage Resistance
              +20% All Out and Crit Modifier
              With rifts up: adds a HoT (scales with rifts)
              --> Use at the start, or mid-fight for healing

  ℹ️  Rift multiplier   1 rift = 0.5  ·  2 = 0.7
                        3 = 1.0  ·  4 or more = 1.5
╰────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╯

🔢 COMBOS
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  Two buffs --> 💡 Enter Infinity (Skill 2 at 8 Deadlock)
                = consistent crits
                The Fourth Dimension (Skill 3 at 8 Deadlock)
                = maximum damage
                Have one of them up before you nuke

  Party rotation --> ✅ Start: 5 → 2
                     Then: 3 → 2 → 3 → 2 → 3 → 2 → 5 → 2 → 3 → 4
                     (The Fourth Dimension is up for the nuke)
                     Best with LoO or LR buffing your haste + crit

  Solo rotation --> ✅ Start: 5 → 2
                    Then: 3 → 2 → 3 → 2 → 3 → 2 → 3 → 2 → 4
                    (Enter Infinity is up for the nuke)
                    More consistent when solo

  💡 Both rotations are timed so the Deadlock
     buff lands right before you nuke

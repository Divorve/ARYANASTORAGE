║                                      ║
║             GREAT THIEF              ║
║                                      ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════╝

  Worth Getting?
  ──────────────
  Yes — flexible class that can go Full DPS or Defensive,
  with setups ready for most ultras.
  Rewards low ping and fast Auto Attack spamming.

  Best Used For:  ⚔️ Full DPS  |  🛡️ Defensive
  (Post Ravenous change)


╔══════════════════════════════════════╗
║               COMBOS                 ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════╝

  ⚔️ Full DPS
  ───────────
  Before fight ····  Use Skill 4 first

  ✅ Rotation ·····  Spam Auto Attack + 2 → 5
                      Use 5 when Hidden Blade is active
                      Use 4 whenever it is off cooldown

  🛡️ Defensive
  ─────────────
  Before fight ····  Use Skill 3 & 4 first

  ✅ Rotation ·····  Spam Auto Attack + 2 → 5
                      Use 5 when Hidden Blade is active
                      Use 3 and 4 whenever off cooldown

  ⚠️ Notes
  ─────────
  1 · Using the Dauntless enchantment? You do
      NOT need to use Skill 4
  2 · Great Thief is at its best with low ping,
      which means faster Auto Attack spamming

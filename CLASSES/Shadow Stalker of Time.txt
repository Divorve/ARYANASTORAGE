📌 OVERVIEW
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  Also covers --> Shadow Walker of Time (SWOT) · Short name: SSOT

  Worth getting? --> Yes — a versatile chrono with high DPS from an infinitely
                     scaling DoT nuke and average farming. Incredibly safe:
                     a great "dodge" support, and with the right timing your
                     team is basically invincible.

  Best for --> 💥 DPS · 🛡️ Dodge support · 🌾 Farming

🔢 SKILLS
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  ⏳ Skill 5 (Temporal Eclipse) changes what the other skills do.
     Each skill below shows both versions.

  Skill 1 --> 💡 Rift of Shadows (auto attack)
              50% Physical · 1.2s (0.6s max haste)
              Adds a Chaos Rift (stacks to 5)
              −3% enemy hit chance per stack
              1st rift starts recording damage (last 10s)
              In 5: −3% enemy dodge and +3% your dodge
              per stack (to 5)
              --> Stack 4 rifts before nuking

  Skill 2 --> 💡 Exude / Devour (15 mana · 2 targets)
              150% · 6s cooldown (3s max haste)
              Outside 5: Physical hit + light DoT
              In 5: Magical light HoT
              --> Extra damage and healing

  Skill 3 --> 💡 Vendetta / Umbral Stigma (30 mana)
              6s cooldown (3s max haste)
              Outside 5: +20% All Out on the enemy
              --> Needed to build bigger nukes
              In 5: uses all rifts for a delayed heavy
              DoT (the nuke), based on the last 10s
              --> The delay carries damage into the
              next rotation

  Skill 4 --> 💡 Silhouette / Shadow Warp (0 mana · 2 targets)
              12s cooldown (6s max haste)
              Hits you for 380% attack power after 2s
              Outside 5: +300% dodge for 4s and taunts
              (Focus) for 4s → protects the party
              In 5: taunts for 4s, big DoT, +30 mana
              --> Avoid in 5 vs hard hitters, you lose
              your dodge for the next rotation

  ⏳ Skill 5 --> Temporal Eclipse (30 mana · 3 targets)
                15s cooldown (7.5s max haste)
                Max haste in almost every case
                (only 30% with no Chaos Rift)
                +80% crit chance · −30% enemy hit chance
                --> Use right before your nuke

  ℹ️  Rift multiplier   1 rift = 0.5  ·  2 = 0.7
                        3 = 1.0  ·  4 or more = 1.5

🔢 COMBOS
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  Main Rotation --> ✅ 4 → 2 → 3 → 5 → 2 → 3
                    --> wait for 5 to expire → repeat
                    Dodge first, 2 + 3 for damage, then 5
                    and 2 + 3 again for nuke + HoT

  DPS (no dodge) --> ✅ 3 → 2 → 5 → 4 → 3 → 2

  ✅ Azalith (Elysium)  5 → 3 → 4 → 2 → 3 → 2

  Ultras --> ⚠️ Manage your 4: it taunts and costs HP.
             Not recommended in pubs

  Chainkilling --> ✅ 4 2 3 5 2 3 2 (boss dies, 3s respawn)
                   4 3 5 2 3 2 (skip the first 2 for respawn)
                   4 2 3 5 2 3 2 (back to this if it survives)
                   --> Needs at least Potent Honor or Vainglory
                   --> Kill the boss with the DoT, or your
                   damage may reset on the next kill
                   --> Stay within 10s between nukes

📘 EXTRA INFORMATION — INFINITE NUKE
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  Most bosses --> ℹ️ A LoO or LR as support is enough.
                  Read on for hard bosses like Azalith.

  Scales with --> ℹ️ All Out / All In · Magic Out / Magic In
                  DoT Out / DoT In

  Passive --> ℹ️ 112% All Out · DoT coefficient 0.2

  Base modifier --> ℹ️ 1.5 (4 rifts) × 0.2 × 2.12 × 1 = 0.636

  The goal --> 💡 Reach 1.0 or more and the DoT grows
               forever. You need about +122% more
               from buffs and potions.

  Buff sources --> ✅ Potent Honor Potion ·· 75%
                   LoO ·················· 30%
                   LR ··················· 30%
                   LH ··················· 25%
                   Bard ················· 20%
                   LC ··················· 15%
                   Vainglory ············ 15%

  Damage cap --> ℹ️ 100 million. Over 122% just gets you
                 there faster. Can one-shot ultras with
                 a boss shield (takes a few minutes)

  ℹ️  Defense debuffs   Arachno, VDK and similar raise the nuke,
                        but aren't counted in the recording

  Why not solo? --> ℹ️ At 0.636 the recorded damage caps out,
                    so it never grows. Above 1.0 every nuke
                    is bigger than the last.

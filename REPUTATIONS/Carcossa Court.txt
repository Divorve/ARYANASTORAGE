⚔️ QUESTS
╭───────────────────────────────── SOLO ─────────────────────────────────╮
  Complete Sigrid's "Weapon of Choice" quest
  ⚠️ Requires: the "There's an Order to These Things" quest

  Aegis Shard                   ×6     --> Aegis of Order
  Sentinal Shard                ×1     --> Sentinel of Order

  Rewards --> 2,500 Gold + 5,000 XP + Doomed Victory ×1
              150 Rep (free version)
              200 Rep + Doomed Victory ×2 (Legend version)

  💡 Best if you're farming alone with a good farming class:
     both drops come from regular monsters, so you can kill them
     in bulk and turn in over and over.
╰──────────────────────────────── GROUP ─────────────────────────────────╮
  Complete Dahlia's "Downfall of Empires" quest
  ⚠️ Requires: the "Three of Kind" quest

  Dahlia's Hairpin              ×1     --> Downfall of Empires (boss)

  Rewards --> 3,000 Gold + 10,000 XP + Doom Lily ×1
              200 Rep

  💡 Best with a group of friends who can nuke the boss instantly:
     one kill = one turn-in.

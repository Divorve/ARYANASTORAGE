📍 LOCATION

  Map --> /join Nerites
╰────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╯

📌 GENERAL
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  NOT a solo boss --> bring friends to help you
  Every hit is True Damage --> dmg resist / defense does nothing
  Hydro Slam and Water Breath ignore Focus --> she can't be taunted
  Heals 500,000 HP after every Water Breath --> you need Decay
  Cannot be stunned --> stun skills do nothing here
  Best class --> Legion Revenant

💥 ATTACKS
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  Hydro Slam    --> 25% of max HP · True Damage · 2.5s CD
                    AoE 7 · Ignores Focus

  Water Breath  --> 70% of max HP · True Damage · 10s CD
                    AoE 7 · Ignores Focus
                    Applies Torrential Terror 4s
                    --> -50% hit chance · -50% outgoing dmg
                    Always followed by Regenerate

  Regenerate    --> Nerites heals 500,000 HP
                    Used right after Water Breath

🧠 STRATEGY
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  Regenerate heals her 500,000 HP --> keep Decay on her at all times
  No Decay class? --> Decay scroll works, but uptime is far from 100%

  All hits are True Damage --> damage resist / defense means nothing
  --> that's why Avarice works so well here

  Water Breath = 70% max HP + Hydro Slam = 25% max HP
  --> be close to full HP when Water Breath comes (every 10s)

  Forge helms --> only use them if you stay above 3k HP

  💡 Dragon-tagged --> damage boosts vs Dragons work here.

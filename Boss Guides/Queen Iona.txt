🧠 STRATEGY
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  🔴 Red zone   = NEGATIVE
  🟢 Green zone = POSITIVE

  NORMAL --> stand in the OPPOSITE zone of your symbol
    ➕ over your head --> go to the 🔴 RED (negative) zone
    ➖ over your head --> go to the 🟢 GREEN (positive) zone

  REVERSED --> boss says everything is reversed / inversed
    --> MATCH your symbol with the zone
    ➕ over your head --> go to the 🟢 GREEN (positive) zone
    ➖ over your head --> go to the 🔴 RED (negative) zone

  You have 4 seconds after Roaring Thunder to get to the right zone
  Wrong zone --> -5000% dmg + magic resist --> you die to the next hit

  70% HP --> Supercell · Ion Shift (reversed) can start happening
  40% HP --> Queen of Skye · she hits much harder from here

  💡 Every hit always crits --> keep your HP high at all times.

📌 OVERVIEW
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  Main goal --> Reach Level 75 so you can farm Warlord Icewing,
                the fastest XP in the game once you get there.

  Golden rule --> Only farm XP and Gold during server boosts.
                  Same effort, far more reward.
╰────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╯

📦 WHAT YOU NEED
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  --> Optional: a friend (ideally end-game) to help you kill monsters
  --> A class with AoE skills (a good farming class)
╰────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╯

🧭 THE ORDER TO DO THINGS
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  1. XP
  2. Chaos Saga
  3. General storyline
  4. Questlines (complete them all)
     ⏳ Some can't be finished right away: their maps are locked to
     seasons of the year (e.g. Frostval in winter / Christmas).
     Skip them for now, but don't forget them. Come back when the
     seasonal event comes around.
  5. Classes / 51% boost weapon
  6. Reputations
  7. End-game content

❄️ WARLORD ICEWING (LV 75+)
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  Map        --> /join icewing  (locked zone · Level 75+ · room limit 7)
                 Also reachable from Icestorm Arena: east of Screen 22,
                 "Warlord Icewing" button from the Warlord Icewing NPC
  Quest      --> "Warlord Icewing!" from King Rimescar
  Requires   --> Warlord Icewing Defeated ×1 (dropped by the boss)

  Rewards    --> Free:   75,000 XP  + 300 Gold  (+ server boost)
                 Member: 100,000 XP + 300 Gold  (+ server boost)

  Difficulty --> VERY easy with a group of friends.
                 Very hard solo: Icewing hits extremely hard.

  Solo?      --> Use a high-DPS class with great dodge, like
                 Timekeeper, CSS, or any Chrono class with strong
                 nukes and high dodge chance.

✨ MID-GAME MUST-HAVES · BLADE + CAPE OF AWE
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  At some point in the mid-game levels, get these two. Blade first,
  then go straight for the Cape.

  [ 1 · BLADE OF AWE ]
  --> +10% damage to Chaos, Dragon, Elemental, Human and Undead
  --> Unlocks the Awe enhancements shop
  --> Needs Rank 6 Blade of Awe rep (Valencia's quests at /join museum)

  [ 2 · CAPE OF AWE ]
  --> +25% Rep, Gold, XP and Class Points when equipped
  --> Merged from a Cape Relic (Binky at /join doomvault)
  ⚠️ Binky is tough: bring a group.

  See: 📖 Armor of Awe (both are covered in this guide)

🗺️ ON THE SIDE · COMPLETE THE STORY
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  Most mid-game and end-game content is LOCKED behind finishing
  earlier maps, so story progress matters as much as your level.

  Track your progress in two places:
  --> Book of Lore: shows how many adventures you've completed
      (goal: e.g. "You have completed 56 of 56 adventures!")
  --> Map --> "Story" tab: every saga, its quest count
      (e.g. Prologue 3/3) and its recommended level range

  💡 Bored of Icewing? Swap to questlines between runs.

  ⏳ Seasonal questlines (like Frostval) only open during their event.
     Skip them until then, but don't forget to come back for them.

  ⚠️ Recommendation: complete ALL of these before worrying
     about anything else.

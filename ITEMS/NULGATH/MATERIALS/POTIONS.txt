📌 OVERVIEW
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  Golden rule --> Don't buy potions. Brew them.

  Potions matter a lot for Ultra fights (like Ultra Darkon), and people
  waste 40-60 million gold buying them. Brewing them yourself costs a
  fraction of that.

  ✅ Only buy the non-alchemy potions, like Endurance Draught,
     Felicitous Philtre and Vigor Potion.

  Credit --> Brewing Potions 101 by u/Epicious (r/AQW)
             https://www.reddit.com/r/AQW/comments/vy0ggh/brewing_potions_101_mainly_offensive_potions/

🧪 HOW POTIONS WORK
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  You can triple buff yourself: use a Tonic, an Elixir and a Potion
  at the same time.

  [ TONIC ]
  --> Raises a base stat (Luck, Wisdom, etc.)
  --> Lasts about 15-20 minutes
  --> Has an Unstable version

  [ ELIXIR ]
  --> Raises a secondary stat (Haste, Physical Damage, etc.)
  --> Lasts about 15-20 minutes
  --> Has an Unstable and a Potent version

  [ POTION ]
  --> A short boost, e.g. more damage for 35 seconds
  --> Equip it for the fight; it may loop depending on your class

  💡 Drink your Tonic and Elixir first, then switch to your potions.
  ⚠️ Potent and Unstable are stronger versions, but Unstable ones have
     drawbacks. Unstable Fate Tonic lowers your Endurance (less HP),
     so it's bad for Ultras.

🐉 DRAGON RUNESTONES
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  Dragon Runestones can be bought for gold, and they make brewing easy:

  --> Skip the brewing mini-game
  --> Give you the Potent version (mostly), some of which can't be bought
  --> Give you ×8 potions (normally 6-10, depending on the potion)

  Example --> Buying Potent Battle Elixir ×8 costs 2 million gold.
              Brewing it takes 1 Dragon Runestone (100k) + 2 reagents.

  💡 Brewing is a bit random, but you could brew 20 times before it
     costs the same as buying, and you get other potions along the way.
╰────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╯

⚗️ HOW BREWING WORKS
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  Each alchemy reagent has a higher chance of giving certain potions.
  Mix 2 reagents and you can get potions from either reagent's pool.

  ⚠️ The wiki lists which potions are possible, but isn't always accurate
     about which reagents give the best chances. The tables below are
     from u/Epicious's own testing.

  See: 📖 Alchemy (reputation guide)

  Arashtite Ore + Dried Slime --> Fate / Sage Tonic
                                  Mobs in Room 2 of /join orecavern
  Doomatter                   --> Battle / Malevolence Elixir
                                  Buy from Swindle (10 = 300k Gold)
  Chaoroot                    --> Honor / Malice Potion
                                  Buy from Swindle (10 = 300k Gold)

🧮 COST EXAMPLE (MAX STACKS)
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮

  Doomatter                 ×30 --> 300k Gold

✅ TL;DR
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  1. Buying potions is a scam. Brew them instead.
  2. Buy Dragon Runestones: Potent potions and no mini-game.
  3. Use the right reagents:
     Arashtite Ore + Dried Slime = Fate / Sage   (/join orecavern, Room 2)
     Doomatter                   = Battle / Malevolence   (Swindle)
     Chaoroot                    = Honor / Malice   (Swindle)

📦 HOW TO ACCESS
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮

✅ STEP 1 — FARM THE CHAMPION SASHES
╭──────────────── "FINAL FIGHT!" quest @ /join celestialarena ───────────╮
  Champion Sash                 ×20    --> Dropped by Aranx (100% drop)

  --> Walk through the blue portal and defeat Aranx

✅ STEP 2 — BUY THE GOLD VOUCHERS
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮

  Gold Voucher 500k             ×3     --> 1,500,000 Gold total

✅ STEP 3 — MERGE THE BLADE
╭────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
  Exchange in Celestial Champion Shop:
  Champion Sash                 ×20    --> Reward from Step 1
  Gold Voucher 500k             ×3     --> Purchased in Step 2
  --> Produces the Burning Blade of Abezeth
╰────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╯

⭐ OPTIONAL — DUAL BURNING BLADES OF ABEZETH
╭───────────── Yulgar's Dual Wield Drop Off @ /join nostalgiaquest ──────╮
  ⚠️ Requires an account with 8 years played

  Unlock the map:
  Book of Lore --> Achievement Badges --> Get the Golden 8th Birthday Candle
  --> This unlocks the /join nostalgiaquest command

  Exchange in Yulgar's Dual Wield Drop Off:
  Burning Blade of Abezeth      ×1     --> Reward from Step 3
  --> Produces the Dual Burning Blades of Abezeth
  --> Same 30% boost as the single blade

  ⚠️ This merge CONSUMES your Burning Blade of Abezeth!
